Card size sets the price of each shipped request

Queue math for a one-active-request development subscription

Flat-fee development subscriptions advertise unlimited requests, and the headline hides the actual constraint: one active request at a time. On a board that runs Requests to Active to Approved, the fee never changes with volume and the stated turnaround is 72 hours per request. Under those rules the one variable you control is how big each card is, and card size sets the price of everything the subscription ships.

Run the arithmetic at the 72-hour claim. A request every three days is ten a month, 120 a year. The yearly plan runs $4,995 a month, $59,940 a year, so each delivered request costs $499.50 at that pace. Ship the same hours as month-long cards and the math inverts: one active slot means one delivered request a month, $4,995 each. Identical fee, identical engineer, ten times the shipped work, decided entirely by how the work was sliced before it entered the queue.

Slicing has a second effect the ledger makes visible: a three-week card that hits a wrong assumption in week one still ships nothing until week three. Fifteen one-day cards across the same business days fail fast instead. The wrong assumption costs one day, and the revision loop has fourteen chances left in the same window, because revisions run until the card is right rather than until the estimate runs out.

Three checks keep the queue honest. First, write the done-test before a card enters Active, because a card that ships and then reopens consumes the slot a second time at full price. Second, cap card size at one business day where the work allows it; a 72-hour turnaround makes three business days the natural ceiling per request. Third, use the roughly 24-hour onboarding to load the board before month one starts, so the first Active slot is never idle waiting on scope.

The model only holds while the fee stays flat as the count scales, which is the whole claim of an unlimited development subscription: pay for the slot, then engineer the slot's throughput. PoketDev prices the slot at $5,995 month to month or $4,995 on the yearly plan, and the table above is the entire game.
